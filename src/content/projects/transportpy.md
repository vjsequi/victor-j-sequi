## Where it started

Transport modelling involves repeated work with matrices, assignment outputs, and network data. Those workflows were an early opportunity for me to connect domain knowledge with Python automation.

## The work

TransportPy is a reusable Python package from this period of my transport-modelling work. It brought specialist processing and automation into a package that could be installed and reused.

The package was published to PyPI in September 2019. It belongs to an earlier stage of my work, when I was moving from individual analyses towards tools that could support repeated tasks.

## The engineering and delivery lesson

Packaging changes the unit of delivery: an analytical script becomes a versioned dependency with an installation path and a defined interface. That creates a reason to separate reusable operations from the assumptions of one analysis.

The business motivation was practical: capture domain knowledge in tooling that could support repeated transport-modelling work. I have not retained a measured productivity result to attach to that motivation.

## Why it still belongs here

The project connects transport engineering, programming, and practical automation. It is an early example of a pattern that continues through my work: understand a domain well enough to make a useful tool for it.

## The result

Published on PyPI in September 2019, TransportPy records an early step from analytical automation to reusable software. It remains a historical project, with no claim of current maintenance or adoption.
